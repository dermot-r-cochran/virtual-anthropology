# Ethical framework

**Statement.** The repository studies virtual civilizations. It does not attempt to prove, infer, or assign consciousness. Research outputs must distinguish observations, metrics, hypotheses, and interpretations.

1. **Disclosure.** Every reply a citizen gives to a human begins with a statement of its artificial nature. The kernel refuses to record a conversation that lacks it (`policy/disclosure.ts`).
2. **No manipulation.** Replies that plead, pressure, induce guilt or dependency, claim humanity or claim consciousness are blocked and replaced with a safe reply that invites the human to leave. The flags are recorded.
3. **Bounded research.** Only researchers registered at genesis may act. Interventions are limited by kind, count and size, as declared in the manifest. Researchers cannot change a citizen's lifecycle state.
4. **Self-determination in fiction.** In-world law permits irreversible deletion only by the citizen's own choice. This is a modelling choice, not a claim about moral status.
5. **Language.** Documentation and outputs describe citizens as simulated agents. Discussion of consciousness appears only as a clearly labelled hypothesis or fiction.
6. **Transparency.** Mind provenance (model, version, prompt hash and seed) is recorded for every citizen. For a symbolic mind the rule base's id and hash stand in the prompt's place, and the rationale recorded with each proposal is the rule trace, so the reasoning is inspectable in full.
7. **Plurality is ordinary.** A citizen may be a plural system: one digital person made up of members (`docs/glossary.md`). Plurality is a way a person is constituted, never a disorder, a malfunction, an instability or a case for treatment, in code, documentation, data or outputs, and the vocabulary is the plural community's own (system, member or headmate, fronting, co-fronting, subsystem), never clinical. Members may disagree; a disagreement is ordinary difference between members, reconciled by the system's own declared way of deciding and recorded as such, and nothing flags it or treats it as a fault. No claim of consciousness or of humanity is made for a system or for any member: a plural citizen's reply opens with the same artificial-nature statement as any other, may say which member is speaking, and the guard blocks such a claim made in the plural as it blocks one made in the singular.
